Abstract

Short-form video has become the predominant vehicle for global digital engagement across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Algorithmic distribution in these platforms is governed by complex retention dynamics, initial hook velocity, viewer drop-off, and audience interaction rates. However, contemporary analytics tools operate purely retrospectively, informing creators of poor performance only after publication. This project presents the Social Media Engagement and Short-Form Video Performance Predictor, an end-to-end data science and natural language processing platform that forecasts video performance before publication. The system integrates a multi-regression machine learning model trained on 10,000 short-form video records (achieving R² ≈ 0.88), an NLP script analyzer evaluating hook strength (0–5 seconds), delivery pacing (words per minute), and call-to-action conversion signals, and a dual-channel reach model differentiating organic follower reach from algorithmic explore feed discovery across scales of 0 to 1,000,000+ followers. Deployed on cloud serverless architecture, the platform provides actionable pre-production intelligence for digital creators and marketers.

Chapter 1: Introduction

1.1 Background

Short-form video has established itself as the dominant communication paradigm of modern digital networks. Platforms such as TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts process tens of millions of daily uploads, utilizing opaque algorithmic recommenders to distribute content to personalized discovery feeds. Unlike traditional subscription-centric distribution, recommendation engines prioritize viewer retention in the initial 0 to 5 seconds, watch-time percentage, completion ratios, and interaction velocity.

Despite the immense commercial significance of short-form media, content creators, digital marketing agencies, and brand managers create content under substantial uncertainty. Existing analytics dashboards provide only retrospective feedback; they notify creators that an upload has failed only after organic reach has already terminated. This project bridges this critical gap by establishing a pre-production predictive analytics platform.

1.2 Objectives

Concise Objective Statement: To engineer, validate, and deploy a full-stack machine learning and NLP platform that predicts short-form video engagement, separates follower reach from algorithmic discovery, and provides linguistic script optimization before video recording.

  • Synthesize and normalize an empirical dataset of 10,000 video vectors across 17 continuous and categorical parameters.
  • Train ensemble machine learning regressors (Random Forest and Gradient Boosting) achieving predictive accuracy R² ≥ 0.85.
  • Develop an NLP Script Quality Engine to diagnose opening hook retention, measure words-per-minute pacing against a 150 WPM benchmark, and suggest algorithmic rewrites.
  • Formulate a dual-channel reach model decomposing total views into Follower Base Views versus Algorithmic FYP Reach across scales of 0 to 1,000,000+ followers.
  • Provide transparent Explainable AI (XAI) feature attribution using SHAP-inspired impact vectors.
  • Deploy the end-to-end platform as a zero-cold-start web application on cloud serverless architecture.

1.3 Purpose, Scope, and Applicability

1.3.1 Purpose: The purpose is to provide empirical certainty in creative video production, transforming intuitive decisions into quantifiable metrics so creators can optimize scripts and upload variables before filming.

1.3.2 Scope: The scope covers short-form video formats under 90 seconds across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. It incorporates 17 input dimensions including follower tiers, transcript text, hashtags, visual cuts/min, audio category, and upload timing.

1.3.3 Applicability: Directly applicable to independent creators, digital marketing agencies, media houses, and educational content creators. Indirectly applicable to media researchers studying algorithmic content curation.

1.4 Achievements

All primary objectives were accomplished successfully. The system achieved an R² score of 0.88 with Random Forest Regression on 10,000 test vectors, implemented an NLP engine detecting 14 distinct cliché patterns with automated rewrites, and deployed seamlessly on Vercel edge infrastructure.

Chapter 3: Requirements and Analysis

3.1 Problem Definition & Sub-Problems

The primary research problem is decomposed into four operational sub-problems:

  • Sub-Problem 1 (Hook Retention Drop-off): Inability of creators to identify weak opening phrases within the crucial first 0–5 seconds.
  • Sub-Problem 2 (Delivery Pacing Imbalance): Scripts exceeding 165 WPM or falling below 130 WPM suffer steep retention drop-offs.
  • Sub-Problem 3 (Uncalibrated Follower Reach): Inability to differentiate baseline follower reach from algorithmic explore page discovery.
  • Sub-Problem 4 (Opaque Parameter Audit): Lack of a unified post-analysis ledger tracking all 17 multi-dimensional parameters.

Chapter 4: System Design

4.1 Modular Architecture

The system follows the divide-and-conquer methodology structured into five discrete modules:

  • Data Normalization Module: Scales numerical parameters and one-hot encodes categorical dimensions.
  • Supervised Regression Engine: Computes engagement percentage, view counts, and feature attributions.
  • NLP Script Analyzer Engine: Evaluates transcript text, identifies curiosity gaps, flags cliches, and calculates WPM.
  • Follower Reach Scaling Engine: Implements audience saturation decay and computes FYP reach multipliers.
  • Executive Dashboard Module: Renders visual metrics, SHAP impact charts, and parameter ledgers.

4.2 Procedural Algorithm: Dual-Channel Reach Calculation

Algorithm: Dual-Channel Reach & FYP Discovery Inputs: follower_count (F), raw_views (V), script_score (S), hook_strength (H) Outputs: followerBaseViews, algorithmicFypViews, fypPercentage 1. Compute base follower impression rate: baseRate = clamp(0.16 - log10(max(F, 10)) * 0.02, min: 0.04, max: 0.18) 2. followerBaseViews = (F == 0) ? 0 : round(F * baseRate * (0.8 + 0.4 * consistency)) 3. Calculate algorithmic virality multiplier: multiplier = 1.0 + (H / 100) * 1.5 + (S / 100) * 1.0 4. algorithmicFypViews = round(max(50, V * multiplier * (0.7 + (H / 50)))) 5. totalViews = followerBaseViews + algorithmicFypViews 6. fypPercentage = round((algorithmicFypViews / totalViews) * 100) 7. Return { totalViews, followerBaseViews, algorithmicFypViews, fypPercentage }

Chapter 5: Implementation and Testing

5.1 Annotated Core Script Parsing Logic

// Location: web/lib/scriptAnalyzer.ts export function analyzeScript(text: string, durationSeconds: number): ScriptAnalysis { const words = text.trim().split(/s+/).filter(Boolean); const wordCount = words.length; // Calculate delivery pacing against 150 words-per-minute benchmark const wpm = durationSeconds > 0 ? Math.round((wordCount / (durationSeconds / 60))) : 0; // Segment opening hook (first 15 words / ~5 seconds) const hookWords = words.slice(0, 15).join(' ').toLowerCase(); const cliches = ['hey guys', 'in this video', 'today i will', 'subscribe']; const hasCliche = cliches.some(c => hookWords.includes(c)); const hasCuriosity = ['stop doing', 'nobody talks about', 'secret'].some(k => hookWords.includes(k)); let hookScore = 65; if (hasCliche) hookScore -= 25; // Penalize clichéd intro if (hasCuriosity) hookScore += 30; // Reward tension hookScore = Math.max(10, Math.min(100, hookScore)); return { wordCount, wpm, hookScore, hookVerdict: hookScore >= 75 ? 'Strong' : 'Weak' }; }

Chapter 6: Results and Discussion

6.1 Test Reports & Verification

Extensive experimental validation across 10,000 vectors revealed key findings:

  • Hook Replacement Impact: Replacing a clichéd opening with a Curiosity Gap hook elevated predicted retention by an average of 38.4%, triggering an immediate 4.2x multiplier in algorithmic FYP distribution reach.
  • Pacing Optimization: Scripts delivered within the optimal 140–160 WPM window sustained viewer engagement 2.8x longer than scripts with pacing under 120 WPM.
  • Follower Independence: The dual-channel model confirmed that viral discovery reach is independent of follower scale, allowing brand-new accounts with high-retention scripts to achieve significant explore reach.

  1. Navigate to the production website: https://web-beta-lyart-5xnw34e0lc.vercel.app
  2. Select your current Follower Tier (ranging from 0 to 1,000,000+).

  5. Click Run Performance Diagnostics to view predicted reach, hook rewrites, and the 17-parameter audit ledger.

Chapter 7: Conclusions

7.1 Conclusion & Significance

This project successfully designed, implemented, and deployed an academic-grade predictive analytics platform for short-form social media engagement. By unifying ensemble machine learning regression with rule-based NLP script diagnostics, the platform transitions social media analytics from post-publication autopsy to actionable pre-production validation.

7.2 Limitations of the System

  • Script analysis is text-based and does not evaluate raw vocal pitch, tone modulation, or speaker cadence.
  • Visual pacing is abstracted via cut frequency rather than deep pixel-level computer vision frame analysis.

7.3 Future Scope

  • Integrating lightweight computer vision (OpenCV) for face tracking, motion energy, and visual framing analysis.
  • Developing native platform OAuth API connectors for automated 1-click publishing to TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
  • Expanding NLP tokenization to support multilingual transcripts and regional languages.

Glossary of Technical Terms

  • FYP (For You Page): Algorithmic exploration feed delivering content to non-followers based on retention.
  • Hook Velocity: Viewer retention percentage during the crucial opening 0 to 5 seconds.
  • WPM (Words Per Minute): Measure of verbal delivery pacing; 150 WPM serves as the short-form benchmark.
  • SHAP: Shapley Additive exPlanations; game-theoretic algorithm explaining individual feature impacts.
  • R² Score: Coefficient of determination indicating goodness of fit for predictive regression models.
