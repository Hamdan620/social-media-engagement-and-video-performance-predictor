Project Synopsis

Social Media Engagement & Short-Form Video Performance Predictor using Machine Learning & NLP

Domain: Machine Learning, NLP & Predictive Analytics
Deployment: Cloud Web Application (Vercel)
Submission: Academic Year 2026

1. Introduction

Short-form video has become the dominant medium of global digital content delivery across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. However, recommendation algorithms prioritize dynamic retention metrics, hook strength, delivery pacing, and audience velocity. Creators and marketing agencies face significant uncertainty regarding video performance. This project delivers an end-to-end data science and machine learning platform that predicts video engagement, decomposes view trajectories into organic follower base vs. algorithmic FYP (For You Page) discovery reach, and performs automated Natural Language Processing (NLP) audits on video scripts.

2. Problem Statement

Existing analytics solutions operate retrospectively—reporting metrics only after content has already been published. Creators lack pre-production predictive intelligence to evaluate whether their video hooks, production pacing, hashtags, audio choices, and upload schedules will capture algorithmic distribution before committing time and financial resources to recording and editing.

3. Project Objectives

  • Pre-Production Engagement Forecasting: Predict engagement rate, expected watch time, shares, comments, and retention rate based on 17+ multi-dimensional creator, production, and scheduling variables.
  • Dual-Channel View Attribution: Quantify audience reach by isolating Follower Base Views from Algorithmic Explore / FYP Discovery Views across follower tiers ranging from 0 to 1,000,000+.
  • NLP Script Quality & Retention Diagnostics: Break down video transcripts into:
    • Hook (0–5 seconds): Detect clichéd openings, identify curiosity gaps, and generate 3 algorithmic rewrites.
    • Body Pacing: Compute words-per-minute (WPM) delivery pacing and flag filler/fluff words.
    • Call-to-Action (CTA): Score comment, share, and save conversion signals.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Provide SHAP-inspired feature attribution to give creators transparent insights into which variables contributed positively or negatively to the final prediction.
  • Cloud Deployment: Deploy an accessible, responsive, academic-grade web application on the cloud.

6. Dataset & Model Training

An exhaustive dataset of 10,000 synthesized and preprocessed short-form video records was developed modeling authentic algorithmic mechanics across 6 niches (Tech, Comedy, Fitness, Education, Finance, Lifestyle) and 3 platforms (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts). Regression modeling utilizing Random Forest and Gradient Boosting achieved high predictive accuracy (R² ≈ 0.88), providing robust generalizability.

7. Expected Outcomes & Practical Value

  • Enables creators to simulate video reception prior to recording, saving substantial creative time and resources.
  • Eliminates weak, clichéd hooks with data-backed alternative suggestions to maximize the critical 0–5 second retention window.
  • Provides realistic audience growth expectations by isolating follower-driven traffic from algorithmic discovery.
